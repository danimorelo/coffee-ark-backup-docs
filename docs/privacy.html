Privacy notes

Only what a backup needs.

This single-owner integration uses Google access to store and verify encrypted backups—not to build profiles, advertise, or provide a public service.

What the utility handles

Database contents are encrypted on Ubuntu before upload. File names, timestamps and sizes remain visible as metadata; encryption does not hide them. The utility uses file names, sizes, hashes, app metadata, ownership and permission information, and downloaded encrypted bytes only for backup delivery, reconciliation and verification.

The recovery identity is kept separately from Ubuntu and Google Drive. A protected refresh credential is stored on Ubuntu; access tokens are held in memory only.

What Google access permits

The exact scope is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: app-created or explicitly selected files. The utility creates its own private backup folder. It does not browse unrelated Drive files, access Gmail, or read unrelated profile data.

Ownership information is used to confirm that the destination belongs to the authenticated account. This scope is not immutable or append-only.

Retention and revocation

Snapshots are retained until the owner removes them. The utility does not automatically delete snapshots, change sharing permissions, or share backups.

The owner can revoke the Google authorization. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not erase snapshots already stored locally or in Drive.

No unrelated use

The utility does not sell data or use it for advertising or unrelated purposes. These static information pages contain no forms, trackers, or signup flow.

Google operates its account and storage services, and GitHub operates this information site's hosting, under their own policies. These notes do not replace those providers' policies or promise legal compliance.
